Weekend Jollof Rice (original demonstration text)

Blend six fresh tomatoes, three red bell peppers, two scotch bonnet peppers and one large onion until smooth. Pour the blend into a pot and boil it down for about twenty minutes until most of the water has evaporated and the mixture thickens.

Heat half a cup of vegetable oil in a separate heavy pot. Add sliced onions and fry until soft, then stir in two tablespoons of tomato paste and fry for five minutes so the paste loses its sour taste. Add the reduced pepper blend and fry for another ten minutes, stirring often so it does not burn.

Season with thyme, curry powder, two bay leaves, stock cubes and salt to taste. Add three cups of washed long-grain parboiled rice and stir until every grain is coated. Pour in enough chicken stock to just cover the rice, then cover the pot with foil and the lid to trap steam.

Cook on low heat for about thirty minutes. Check occasionally and add small amounts of stock if the rice is still hard when the liquid has dried up. Do not stir too often, because constant stirring breaks the grains and makes the rice mushy.

When the rice is soft, turn up the heat for two or three minutes to create a slightly smoky layer at the bottom of the pot. Many people consider this party flavour the best part of the dish. Remove the bay leaves, fluff the rice with a fork and serve with fried plantain, coleslaw and grilled chicken.

Leftovers keep well in the fridge for up to three days. Reheat with a splash of water so the rice does not dry out. The dish also freezes well in portions, which makes it a convenient option for busy weeks during the semester.
