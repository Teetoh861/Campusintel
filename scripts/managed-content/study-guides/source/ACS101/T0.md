# ACS 101 Study Guide by CampusIntell

**Introduction to Actuarial Science**

**Study Guide · 100L · First Semester · 3 Units**

## Course outline

| Topic | Title |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Nature of actuarial science and the actuary |
| 2 | History and professional structure of actuarial science |
| 3 | Actuarial symbols, notations and life tables |
| 4 | Actuarial science, insurance and the role of actuaries |
| 5 | Time value of money: compound interest and discounting |
| 6 | Present and accumulated values of payment streams |
| 7 | Annuities and annuity functions |

## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Describe the relationship between actuarial science and insurance.
2. Define the various actuarial symbols.
3. Explain the dynamic role of actuarial science as a discipline.
4. Identify the various career choices for an actuary.
5. Solve simple and compound interest problems.
6. Explain the different features of a life table.
7. Define the different types of annuities and state their formulae.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT), with some theory questions. Expect definitions, history, actuarial symbols, life-table values and calculations.
- Calculations come from Topics 3, 5, 6 and 7. Practise the formulas until you can do them quickly.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all seven.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for worked examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
