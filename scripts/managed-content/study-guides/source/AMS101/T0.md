## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Explain who managers are, what they do, and how management thinking developed.
2. Describe the environment managers work in, including culture, globalization, social responsibility and ethics.
3. Apply the decision-making process and explain how managers plan and choose strategies.
4. Describe the elements of organizational structure and compare common organizational designs.
5. Outline the human resource management process, from planning and hiring to appraisal and pay.
6. Explain why organizations change, how people react to change, and how managers encourage innovation.
7. Explain what shapes individual behaviour and how groups and teams work.
8. Compare the main theories of motivation and how managers reward employees.
9. Compare the main theories of leadership and explain how managers communicate and manage information.
10. Explain the control process and the basics of operations management.

## How you are assessed

- Continuous assessment (tests and assignments): 30 to 40 marks.
- Final exam: 60 to 70 marks, lasting 2 to 3 hours.
- The exam is mostly objective (CBT). Be ready for some theory questions too, where you define, list and explain.
- You need at least 65% lecture attendance to sit the exam.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for the full picture and examples.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question.
- Then practise. Each topic ends with a link to its practice questions, with an explanation for every answer.
