## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Define marketing and the marketing mix elements.
2. Explain the marketing concepts and how they are practised.
3. Identify the key forces in the marketing environment.
4. Explain how consumers and organisations make buying decisions.
5. Explain the marketing system and the marketing management process.
6. Design and apply a market segmentation, targeting and positioning approach.
7. Measure market demand and prepare simple sales forecasts.
8. Explain the place of digital and social media in modern marketing.
9. Apply marketing concepts, principles and theories to solve marketing problems.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT), with some theory questions. Expect definitions, lists, counts, classifications and short applications to Nigerian businesses.
- A few questions need simple calculations: market potential, market share, forecasts, channel contacts and digital marketing metrics (Topics 6 to 10).
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all ten.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
