## Topic 2: Algebraic, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

**What you must know:** functions, domain, range and composites; straight lines and linear business models; equations, identities, inequalities, quadratics and simultaneous equations; the vertex of a parabola in revenue, profit and cost problems; laws of exponents and logarithms; exponential growth and decay models.

### Lesson 2.1: Functions, domain, range and composites

**Must know**

- **Function:** a rule that assigns to each element x in the domain D **exactly one** element f(x) in the range R (two x values may share a value; one x may not have two).
- **Domain:** the x values for which the function can be evaluated. **Range:** the values f(x) takes. x is **independent**, y **dependent**.
- **Domain rules:** denominator ≠ 0; inside an even root ≥ 0; inside ln or log > 0. Price and quantity cannot be negative.
- **Composite:** g[h(x)] puts h(x) wherever u appears in g(u); (f o g)(x) = f[g(x)], inside first.
- **Piecewise:** use the formula whose interval contains x. **Two variables:** z = f(x, y), domain = all workable pairs.

**Explain more**

- **Evaluate:** f(x) = x^3 − 2x^2 + 3x + 100, f(2) = 8 − 8 + 6 + 100 = **106**.
- **Domains:** 1/(x − 3): all x **except 3**. sqrt(x − 2): **x ≥ 2**. e^(sqrt x) + ln y: **x ≥ 0, y > 0**, and f(0, 1) = e^0 + ln 1 = **1**.
- **Business domain:** R(p) = p(600 − 20p) needs p ≥ 0 and q = 600 − 20p ≥ 0, so **0 ≤ p ≤ 30**.
- **Range:** S(t) = 500 − 300e^(−0.4t), t ≥ 0: S(0) = 200, and S rises towards 500 without reaching it: **200 ≤ S < 500**.
- **Composite:** g(u) = u^2 + 3u + 1, h(x) = x + 1. g[h(x)] = (x + 1)^2 + 3(x + 1) + 1 = **x^2 + 5x + 5**. f(x) = 2x + 3, g(x) = x^2 − 1: g(3) = 8, f(8) = **19**.
- **In context:** C(p) = 0.5p + 1, p(t) = 10 + 0.1t^2 give C(t) = **6 + 0.05t^2**; C = 6.8 gives t^2 = 16, **t = 4 years**. q(t) = 25t, C(q) = 0.1q^2 + 12q + 5,000: 8 hours gives q = 200, C = 4,000 + 2,400 + 5,000 = **₦11,400**.
- **Change:** C(x) = 20,000 + 150x + 0.4x^2: C(60) − C(50) = 30,440 − 28,500 = **₦1,940**.
- **Piecewise:** commission 6% up to ₦300,000, else ₦6,000 + 4%. On ₦500,000: 6,000 + 20,000 = **₦26,000** (₦30,000 uses the wrong piece).
- **Tiered bill:** ₦1.22, ₦10, ₦50 per 100 cubic feet for the first 1,200, next 1,200, and the rest. 3,000 cubic feet: 14.64 + 120 + 300 = **₦434.64**.
- **Building a model** (write the quantity, then remove the extra variable using the constraint):
    - Picnic area xy = 5,000, side x unfenced: F = x + 2y = **x + 10,000/x**.
    - Can, volume 24π: h = 24/r^2. Ends 2πr^2 × ₦3 = 6πr^2; side 2πrh × ₦2 = 96π/r. **C(r) = 6πr^2 + 96π/r**.
    - Cable, river 900 m, factory 3,000 m downstream: water sqrt(810,000 + x^2) at ₦5, land (3,000 − x) at ₦4. **C(x) = 4(3,000 − x) + 5 sqrt(810,000 + x^2)**.

### Lesson 2.2: Linear functions and straight-line models

**Must know**

- **Linear function:** y = mx + b; **constant rate** of change; straight-line graph.
- **Slope:** m = (y2 − y1)/(x2 − x1), the change in y per unit rise in x. **y-intercept:** b, y at x = 0.
- **Point-slope form:** y − y0 = m(x − x0).
- To read slope and intercept, make y the subject: 5y + 2x = 20 gives y = −(2/5)x + 4, slope **−2/5**, intercept **4**.
- **Cost line:** C(x) = fixed cost + (variable cost per unit)x. In C(x) = 45,000 + 350x, ₦45,000 is the fixed cost and each extra unit adds ₦350.

**Explain more**

- Slope through (−3, 4) and (5, −8): (−8 − 4)/(5 − (−3)) = −12/8 = **−1.5**. A line with slope −3.1 and intercept 10: **y = −3.1x + 10**.
- **Rising price:** bread rises ₦2 a month, ₦106 on 1 November (x = 10 months after 1 January): 106 = 20 + b, so p = 2x + 86, **₦86** on 1 January. Soft drink ₦156: p = 2x + **136**. Petrol ₦103 on 1 June, 1 October is 4 months later: **₦111**.
- **Two points:** reservoir 200 (day 12), 164 (day 21): slope −4 a day, day 8 = **216 million gallons**. Scores 575 (1986), 545 (1991): −6 a year, 1996 = **515**.
- **Cost:** ₦85,000 for 100 copies, ₦130,000 for 250: m = 45,000/150 = 300, b = 85,000 − 30,000: **C = 300x + 55,000**.
- **Straight-line depreciation:** ₦2,400,000 to ₦1,500,000 in 3 years is ₦300,000 a year; zero after **8 years**.
- **Linear demand:** 1,200 bags at ₦500, 900 at ₦650: slope −2, x = 2,200 − 2p, zero at **₦1,100**.
- **Two plans:** A = 25 + 0.60x, B = 30 + 0.50x. Equal when 0.1x = 5, **x = 50**. A (lower fixed charge) is cheaper below 50, B (lower rate) above. A lower rate is not always cheaper.

### Lesson 2.3: Equations, identities, inequalities and systems

**Must know**

- **Equation:** true only for **particular values** of its variable(s). **Identity:** true for **all values**, e.g. (x + 2)^2 = x^2 + 4x + 4, x^2 + 2x = x(x + 2).
- **Inequality:** two expressions joined by >, <, ≥ or ≤, e.g. 3x + 1 > 0.
- **Degree** = highest power (2x^5 − 3x^2 + 1 = 0 is degree 5; 5 − 6x + 4x^7 − 2x^10 is degree 10). 1 linear, 2 quadratic, 3 cubic, 4 bi-quadratic.
- **Linear equation:** no powers above 1, no products of variables. **System of linear equations:** e.g. 2x + 3y = 7 and x − y = 1 (x^2, xy or 1/y make it non-linear).
- Multiplying or dividing an inequality by a **negative** number **reverses** the sign.
- **Quadratic formula:** ax^2 + bx + c = 0 gives x = (−b ± sqrt(b^2 − 4ac))/(2a). **Discriminant** b^2 − 4ac: > 0 two real roots; = 0 one repeated root; < 0 no real roots.
- **Roots α, β:** α + β = −b/a, αβ = c/a. The equation with roots α and β is x^2 − (α + β)x + αβ = 0.
- Useful identities: α^2 + β^2 = (α + β)^2 − 2αβ; x^2 − y^2 = (x + y)(x − y).
- **Systems:** elimination or substitution (substitution when one equation is non-linear).

**Explain more**

- **Inequalities:** x − 9 > 3x + 1 gives −2x > 10, flip: **x < −5**. 4(3x + 2) ≥ 2x gives **x ≥ −4/5**. 5(3 − 2x) < 3(4 − 3x) gives −x < −3, **x > 3**.
- **Word inequality:** x pencils at ₦5, (x + 4) pens at ₦8, total ≤ ₦97: 13x + 32 ≤ 97, x ≤ **5**.
- **Quadratics:** x^2 − 8x + 15 = (x − 3)(x − 5), x = **3 or 5**. 2x^2 − 5x = 6x + 10 becomes 2x^2 − 11x − 10 = 0: x = (11 ± sqrt(121 + 80))/4 = **(11 ± sqrt 201)/4**. x^2 − 10x − 9 = 0: x = (10 ± sqrt 136)/2 = **5 ± sqrt 34**.
- **Forming equations:** roots of x^2 − 3x − 10 = 0 have a + b = 3, ab = −10. For roots a^2, b^2: sum = 9 − 2(−10) = 29, product = (ab)^2 = 100, giving **x^2 − 29x + 100 = 0**. For 2x^2 − 4x + 1 = 0 (a + b = 2, ab = 1/2), roots a^2 + b and a + b^2: sum = (a^2 + b^2) + (a + b) = (4 − 1) + 2 = 5; product = a^3 + a^2b^2 + ab + b^3 = (a + b)^3 − 3ab(a + b) + (ab)^2 + ab = 8 − 3 + 1/4 + 1/2 = 23/4. Equation x^2 − 5x + 23/4 = 0, i.e. **4x^2 − 20x + 23 = 0**.
- **Fractions:** (x + 3)/(x + 2) + (x − 3)/(x − 2) = (2x − 3)/(x − 1). Left side = (2x^2 − 12)/(x^2 − 4); cross-multiply and simplify: x^2 − 4x = 0, **x = 0 or 4**.
- **Elimination:** 2x + y = 11, 3x + 5y = 27. Five times the first minus the second: 7x = 28, **x = 4, y = 3**.
- **Substitution (non-linear):** x + y = 5 and x^2 + y^2 = 25. Since (x + y)^2 = 25, 2xy = 0: **(0, 5) or (5, 0)**. 2x + 3y = 5, xy = 1: y = 1/x gives 2x^2 − 5x + 3 = 0, x = 1 or 3/2: **(1, 1) or (3/2, 2/3)**. 3x^2 − 5x − 3y = −4 with 3y = 10 − 2x: 3x^2 − 3x − 6 = 0, x = 2 or −1: **(2, 2) or (−1, 4)**.
- **Word systems:**
    - ₦10,000 in bonds at 7% and 10% earning ₦820: x + y = 10,000, 0.07x + 0.10y = 820, so −0.03x = −180: **₦6,000 at 7%, ₦4,000 at 10%**.
    - Chairs (20 min, ₦2), tables (30 min, ₦1), using 600 min and ₦40: 20x + 30y = 600, 2x + y = 40: **15 chairs, 10 tables**.
    - Digits a + b = 12; reversing loses 18, so 9(a − b) = 18, a − b = 2: **75**.
    - Batsman (198 runs in x matches, then 42 more, so 240 in x + 1): 240/(x + 1) = 198/x + 2 clears to x^2 − 20x + 99 = 0, **x = 9 or 11**.
    - 27^x = 9^y gives 3x = 2y; 81 = 243 × 3x gives x = 1/9, y = 1/6.

### Lesson 2.4: Quadratic functions: vertex, intercepts and business use

**Must know**

- y = ax^2 + bx + c is a **parabola**: opens **upward** if a > 0 (vertex is a minimum), **downward** if a < 0 (vertex is a maximum).
- **Vertex:** x = −b/(2a); substitute to get y.
- **x-intercepts:** solve ax^2 + bx + c = 0. **y-intercept:** c.
- **Revenue** R = xp. **Profit** P = R − C. **Break-even:** P = 0 (R = C). For P(x) = ax^2 + bx + c with a < 0, the vertex gives **maximum profit** and the roots are the **break-even outputs**.

**Explain more**

- y = x^2 − 6x + 4: vertex x = 6/2 = 3, y = 9 − 18 + 4 = −5, vertex **(3, −5)**, minimum. Intercepts: x = (6 ± sqrt(36 − 16))/2 = **3 ± sqrt 5**.
- **Maximum revenue:** R = 1,200x − 4x^2: x = 1,200/8 = 150, R = **₦90,000**.
- **Best price:** p = 900 − 3x, R = 900x − 3x^2: x = 150, p = **₦450** (₦67,500 is the revenue).
- **Break-even:** −2x^2 + 240x − 4,000 = 0 becomes x^2 − 120x + 2,000 = 0: **x = 20 and 100** (maximum profit halfway, x = 60).
- **Minimum average cost:** AC = 0.5x^2 − 40x + 1,000: x = 40, AC = 800 − 1,600 + 1,000 = **₦200 per unit**.
- **No break-even:** P = −x^2 + 40x − 500 (₦ thousands). Vertex x = 20, P = −400 + 800 − 500 = −100. The best result is a **loss of ₦100,000**, and b^2 − 4ac = 1,600 − 2,000 < 0, so the firm never breaks even.

### Lesson 2.5: Exponents and logarithms

**Must know**

- **Laws of exponents:** a^0 = 1; a^x · a^y = a^(x+y); a^x / a^y = a^(x−y); (a^x)^y = a^(xy); (ab)^x = a^x b^x; (a/b)^x = a^x / b^x; a^(−n) = 1/a^n; a^(m/n) = (n-th root of a)^m.
- **Logarithm:** log\_b N = x means **b^x = N** (b > 0, b ≠ 1, N > 0).
- **Common logarithm** (Briggsian): base 10, written log x. **Natural logarithm** (Napierian, after John Napier): base e ≈ 2.71828, written ln x. No base written means base 10.
- **Relation:** ln x = log x / log e = log x / 0.4343 = **2.3026 log x**.
- **Laws of logarithms:**
    1. log\_a 1 = 0
    2. log\_a a = 1
    3. log(MN) = log M + log N
    4. log(M/N) = log M − log N
    5. log(M^k) = k log M
    6. log\_a b = 1/log\_b a
- **Change of base:** log\_a N = log N / log a = ln N / ln a
- **Not a law:** log(M + N) ≠ log M + log N.
- **Inverses:** e^(ln x) = x, ln(e^x) = x, 10^(log x) = x. So 10^(log 5) = 5.
- **ln x:** defined only for x > 0; ln 1 = 0; negative for 0 < x < 1, positive for x > 1.

**Explain more**

- **Exponents:** (2^3)^2 = 64; 121^0.5 = 11; 16^(−3/4) = 1/2^3 = **1/8**. 32^(3/5) × 125^(−2/3) = 2^3 × 5^(−2) = **8/25**. 3x·x^(−2)y^5/(4x^4 y^2) = **3y^3/(4x^5)**. [1 − {1 − (1 − x^3)^(−1)}^(−1)]^(1/3): inner bracket −x^3/(1 − x^3), reciprocal −(1 − x^3)/x^3, 1 minus that = 1/x^3, cube root **1/x**. 10^(1/5) × 10^(1/3) ÷ (10^(−2/3) × 10^(−1)) = 10^2.2 = **158.49**.
- **Same base:** 4^(x+1) = 8^(x−1) gives 2^(2x+2) = 2^(3x−3), so **x = 5**.
- **By definition:** log\_6(6 sqrt 6) = log\_6 6^(3/2) = **3/2**. log\_5(cube root 5 × sqrt 5) = 1/3 + 1/2 = **5/6**. log\_x 25 = 2: **x = 5**. log\_x 81 = 4: **x = 3**. (2 sqrt 5)^4 = 400, so log\_(2 sqrt 5) 400 = **4**. log\_2 sqrt 6 + log\_2 sqrt(2/3) = log\_2 sqrt 4 = **1**.
- **Nested logs** (inside out): log\_3 27^3 = 9, log\_3 9 = 2, log\_2 2 = 1, log\_2 1 = **0**. Reverse: log\_2{log\_3(log\_2 x)} = 1 gives log\_3(log\_2 x) = 2, log\_2 x = 9, **x = 512**.
- **Log equations** (reject answers making a log argument or base invalid): log\_2(x + 3) + log\_2(x − 3) = 4 gives x^2 − 9 = 16, **x = 5 only** (−5 rejected). log\_x(4x + 3) − log\_x 4 = 2 gives 4x^2 − 4x − 3 = 0, **x = 3/2 only** (no negative base). log\_x 3 + log\_x 9 + log\_x 729 = 9 log\_x 3 = 9: **x = 3**.
- **Expanding:** log x = 0.5, log y = 1.5, log z = 3: log(xy/z^(1/3)) = 0.5 + 1.5 − 1 = **1**. log a = 0.3, log b = 2.8642, log c = 1.7642: log(a^3 b^(1/2)/c) = 0.9 + 1.4321 − 1.7642 = **0.5679**. ln(x^3 sqrt y/z^2) = **3 ln x + (1/2) ln y − 2 ln z**.
- **From log 2 = 0.3010, log 3 = 0.4771:** log 7.2 = log(2^3 × 3^2/10) = 0.9030 + 0.9542 − 1 = **0.8572**. log\_8 25 = 2 log 5/(3 log 2) = 2(0.6990)/0.9030 = **1.548** (log 5 = 1 − log 2).
- **Simplify:** 7 log(16/15) + 5 log(25/24) + 3 log(81/80). Write in primes: 16/15 = 2^4/(3·5), 25/24 = 5^2/(2^3·3), 81/80 = 3^4/(2^4·5). Powers of 2: 28 − 15 − 12 = 1; of 3: −7 − 5 + 12 = 0; of 5: −7 + 10 − 3 = 0. Result **log 2**.
- **Solving a^x = N:** x = log N/log a. 3^x = 8: 0.9031/0.4771 = **1.893**. 9^x = 2: **0.3155**. 10^x = 8: **0.9031**. log\_5 40 = ln 40/ln 5 = **2.2920**; log\_4 7 = **ln 7/ln 4**.
- **Trap:** 3(2^x) = 48 gives 2^x = 16, x log 2 = log 16, so x = log 16 **÷** log 2 = 4; writing log 16 − log 2 is the error.
- 10,000(1 + i)^10 = 30,000: 1 + i = 3^(0.1), **i = 0.1161**. ln(1 + x) = −0.5: x = e^(−0.5) − 1 = **−0.3935**.
- **Mixed bases:** 2^x · 3^(2x+1) = 7^(4x+3). Logs: x log 2 + (2x + 1) log 3 = (4x + 3) log 7, so x = (3 log 7 − log 3)/(log 2 + 2 log 3 − 4 log 7) = 2.0582/(−2.1252) = **−0.9685**.
- **Quadratic in e^x:** e^(2x) − 5e^x + 6 = 0; u = e^x gives u = 2 or 3, x = **0.6931 or 1.0986**.
- **Computing with logs:** 1000(1.06)^110.5: log = 3 + 110.5 × 0.025306 = 5.79631, antilog ≈ **625,600**. 789.45^(1/8): 2.89732 ÷ 8 = 0.36217, antilog **2.3023**.

### Lesson 2.6: Exponential functions and growth and decay models

**Must know**

- **Exponential function:** f(x) = b^x where the base b is a positive constant (b ≠ 1) and the **variable is the exponent**. x^10 is not exponential (the variable is the base; that is a power function).
- **Graph of b^x:** passes through **(0, 1)**; **positive for every x** (never negative); increasing if b > 1, decreasing if 0 < b < 1; tends to 0 on one side.
- **e** = limit of **(1 + 1/n)^n** as n grows without bound ≈ 2.71828.
- **Discrete growth:** y = a(1 + r)^t (a = starting value, r = rate per period). **Decay (reducing balance):** y = a(1 − r)^t.
- **Continuous growth:** y = ae^(kt), k > 0; **decay** y = ae^(−kt). Equivalent annual rate: r = e^k − 1.
- **Doubling time:** discrete t = ln 2/ln(1 + r); continuous t = ln 2/k. **Half-life:** t = ln 2/k. **Tripling:** replace 2 with 3.
- **Rate from two values:** k = ln(y2/y1)/(t2 − t1); r = (y2/y1)^(1/(t2 − t1)) − 1.
- **Log-linear:** if y = ae^(kt), then ln y = ln a + kt, a straight line with **slope k** and intercept **ln a**.
- **Saturation models:** y = M(1 − e^(−kt)) starts at 0 and rises towards M but never passes it.

**Explain more**

- **Reading a model:** N(t) = 25,000(1.06)^t: 25,000 customers now, growing **6%** a year (not a fixed 1,500). 500e^(−0.3t) is decay; 500 − 0.3t is linear.
- **Discrete:** Ogun State town, 250,000 in 2020 at 3%: 2026 (t = 6) gives 250,000(1.03)^6 = **298,513**. ₦4,500,000 van losing 15% a year: 4,500,000(0.85)^4 = **₦2,349,028**.
- **Doubling and tripling:** 4% a year: ln 2/ln 1.04 = **17.67 years**. Tripling at 9%: ln 3/ln 1.09 = **12.75 years**. 5,000e^(0.08t) doubles in ln 2/0.08 = **8.66 months**.
- **Continuous:** 2,000e^(0.12 × 5) = **3,644 units**. V0 e^(−0.25t) halves in ln 2/0.25 = **2.77 years**. Halving every 5 years: k = ln 2/5 = **0.1386**. ae^(0.08t): r = e^0.08 − 1 = **8.33%**.
- **Rate from data:** ₦40 million (2021) to ₦58 million (2025): k = ln 1.45/4 = **9.29%**. Rice ₦38,000 (2023) to ₦64,000 (2026): r = (64/38)^(1/3) − 1 = **18.98%**. GDP ₦100 billion (1990) to ₦165 billion (2000) is a factor 1.65 per decade: 2010 = **₦272.25 billion**.
- **Time to a level:** 1.2 million (2015), 1.5 million (2020): k = ln 1.25/5 = 0.04463; 2 million at t = ln(2/1.2)/0.04463 = **11.45 years**.
- **Base 10:** log y = 3 + 0.05t gives growth factor 10^0.05 = 1.1220, so **12.20%** a year.
- **Advertising response:** R = 1 − e^(−0.05t), t = 10: 1 − 0.6065 = **39.35%**.
- **Credit collections:** C = 0.92(1 − e^(−0.1t)), t = 3: 0.92(1 − 0.7408) = **23.84%**; as t grows, C approaches **92%**, never 100%.
- **Malt drink:** 50,000(1 − e^(−0.3t)) = 40,000 gives e^(−0.3t) = 0.2, t = ln 5/0.3 = **5.36 months**.

### One-minute recap

- A function gives each x exactly one value; domain excludes zero denominators, negative even-root arguments and non-positive log arguments.
- Composite g[h(x)]: substitute h(x) for u; work inside out.
- Line: y = mx + b, slope = rise/run; fixed cost is the intercept, variable cost the slope.
- Equation: true for some values; identity: true for all. Dividing an inequality by a negative flips the sign.
- Quadratic formula, sum −b/a and product c/a of roots, x^2 − (sum)x + product = 0.
- Vertex at x = −b/(2a): maximum revenue or profit, minimum average cost; profit roots are break-even points.
- log\_b N = x means b^x = N; ln = base e (Napierian), log = base 10 (Briggsian); ln x = 2.3026 log x; log(M + N) is not log M + log N.
- y = a(1 + r)^t and y = ae^(kt); doubling ln 2/ln(1 + r) or ln 2/k; r = e^k − 1; ln y against t is a line of slope k.

### Theory practice

**Question:** A generator bought for ₦1,200,000 loses 20% of its value each year (reducing balance). After how many whole years does its value first fall below ₦400,000?

*Solution:* V = 1,200,000(0.8)^t < 400,000 gives (0.8)^t < 1/3. Take logs: t ln 0.8 < −ln 3. Since ln 0.8 is negative, dividing flips the sign: t > ln 3/(−ln 0.8) = 1.0986/0.2231 = 4.92. So the value first falls below ₦400,000 after **5 years**. Check: after 4 years V = ₦491,520; after 5 years V = ₦393,216.
