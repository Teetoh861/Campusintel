## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Integrate functions and use integration to find totals from marginal functions, areas, and consumer's and producer's surplus.
2. Use algebraic, exponential and logarithmic functions to model and solve business and economics problems.
3. Differentiate functions and interpret marginal cost, revenue, profit and elasticity.
4. Find maximum and minimum values, including with two variables and constraints, and use growth and decay models.
5. Find market equilibrium and break-even points and analyse cost, revenue and profit.
6. Calculate simple and compound interest, present values, annuities and loan repayments.
7. Work with matrices, determinants and inverses, and solve systems of equations.
8. Use arithmetic and geometric progressions in business problems.
9. Apply set operations and Venn diagrams to counting problems.
10. Model consumption, saving and market penetration.

## How you are assessed

- Expect objective questions, mostly short calculations, plus some definitions and rules.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all ten, and work through every worked example with a calculator.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for worked examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
