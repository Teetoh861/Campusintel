## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Define entrepreneurship and describe its scope and dimensions, and the characteristics of an entrepreneur.
2. Explain the traits of an entrepreneur and whether entrepreneurs are born or made.
3. Define innovation and its dimensions, link knowledge to innovation, and apply the principles of change management.
4. Identify the forms of business ownership in Nigeria, weigh their advantages and disadvantages, and outline a simple business plan.
5. Explain business registration in Nigeria, its process and the pitfalls to avoid, and the forms of business collaboration.
6. Define and distinguish micro and small enterprises, and explain their importance.
7. Explain the role of knowledge and skills, digital technology and e-commerce in modern entrepreneurship.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT). Expect definitions, lists and their order, names, figures and Nigerian business cases.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all seven.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
