## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Explain managerial economics and the goals of managerial decisions in profit-seeking, non-profit and public organisations.
2. Apply opportunity cost to business decisions and tell accounting profit from economic profit.
3. Analyse market attractiveness and demand, calculate elasticities, and explain the product life cycle.
4. Describe forecasting types and methods and prepare simple forecasts.
5. Analyse short-run and long-run costs and their implications for managers.
6. Explain market segmentation, targeting and positioning.
7. Compare the types of business firm.
8. Compare market structures and how price and output are set in each.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT), with some theory questions. Expect definitions, lists, applications to Nigerian businesses and short calculations.
- Calculations come mainly from Topics 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8. Practise each worked example until you can do it quickly.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all eight.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for worked examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
