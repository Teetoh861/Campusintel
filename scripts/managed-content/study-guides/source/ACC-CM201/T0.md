## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Explain the purpose of the Conceptual Framework, the qualitative characteristics, the elements, recognition and measurement.
2. Apply the five-step model of IFRS 15 to recognise revenue.
3. Prepare and present financial statements in line with IAS 1.
4. Measure inventories at the lower of cost and net realisable value under IAS 2.
5. Account for changes in accounting policies and estimates, and correct prior period errors, under IAS 8.
6. Recognise, measure, depreciate, revalue and derecognise property, plant and equipment under IAS 16.
7. Account for government grants under IAS 20.
8. Capitalise borrowing costs on qualifying assets under IAS 23.

## How you are assessed

- Expect objective questions and calculations on each standard.
- Calculations come up in every topic. Work through every worked example until you can do them quickly.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all eight.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for worked examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
