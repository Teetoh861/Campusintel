# ACC 101 Study Guide by CampusIntell

**Introduction to Financial Accounting I**

**Study Guide · 100L · First Semester · All Management Sciences departments**

## Course outline

| Topic | Title |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The nature and scope of accounting |
| 2 | Double entry and the accounting equation |
| 3 | The accounting process: source documents, books of original entry and the ledger |
| 4 | Accounting concepts, conventions and limitations |
| 5 | Cash book and bank reconciliation |
| 6 | The trial balance and correction of errors |
| 7 | End-of-year adjustments |
| 8 | Final accounts of a sole trader |

## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Describe the nature and scope of accounting and the objectives of financial accounting.
2. Tell bookkeeping and accounting apart, and name the branches of accounting.
3. Explain the methods of recording accounting data, manual and electronic.
4. Prepare basic accounting records from the books of original entry to the trial balance.
5. Correct basic posting errors.
6. Prepare a bank reconciliation statement.
7. Make the end-of-year adjustments and prepare the final accounts of a sole trader.

## How you are assessed

- Continuous assessment: 30 to 40 marks. Final exam: 60 to 70 marks.
- Expect objective questions on definitions, rules and concepts, and short computations: debits and credits, bank reconciliation, depreciation, adjustments and profit.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all eight.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for the worked examples and the traps.
- Work the examples yourself with a pen before you read the answer.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the practice question, then do that topic's practice questions.
