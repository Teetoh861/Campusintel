**Legal Methods I**

**Study Guide · 100L · First Semester · 3 Units**

## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Define law, explain the schools of thought and assess their relevance today.
2. Explain the objectives of law, the meanings and types of justice, and the relationship between the state, rulers and citizens.
3. Classify law into its main divisions.
4. Explain the techniques by which law is used to control society, and their limits.
5. Explain how facts are found and disputes resolved, in court and outside it.
6. Use the language of legal reasoning: logic, rhetoric, principles, standards and precedent.
7. Describe how legislation is made, its types, and the rules of statutory interpretation with cases.
8. Identify and explain the sources of Nigerian law.
9. Carry out basic legal research and write a structured legal essay or problem answer.

## How you are assessed

- Expect essay questions (for example, answer four out of six) and objective questions.
- Essay questions often quote a jurist or judge and ask you to discuss, with authorities. Learn the definitions with who said them, the lists, and at least one case for each point.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all nine.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for how each point is used in an answer, and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the essay practice question, then do that topic's practice questions.
