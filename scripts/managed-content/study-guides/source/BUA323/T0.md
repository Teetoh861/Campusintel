## Course outcomes

By the end of these topics you should be able to:

1. Define supply chain management, logistics and the value chain, and explain how they differ.
2. Trace a supply chain from supplier to final consumer and explain how value is added at each stage.
3. Explain how to select the right supplier.
4. Describe the scope of logistics and SCM and its six elements: customers, planning, purchasing, inventory, production and transportation.
5. Calculate the economic order quantity and explain the bullwhip effect.
6. State the functions, goals and components of SCM.
7. Explain green supply chain management, its practices, drivers, benefits and challenges.

## How you are assessed

- Expect objective questions and short theory questions: definitions, lists, differences, short calculations and Nigerian examples.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all four.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
