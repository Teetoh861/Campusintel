## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Define and explain the key operating elements and functions of business organisations.
2. Appraise the core management functions (planning, organising, leading and controlling) and show how they relate.
3. Apply foundational management knowledge to marketing, accounting, finance, human resources and operations.
4. Explain the impact of the internal and external environment on modern business, with particular reference to Nigeria.
5. Explain the forms of business ownership, their advantages, limitations and regulation.
6. Analyse the roles, skills and responsibilities of managers as change agents and leaders.
7. Evaluate corporate governance principles and ethical leadership practices.
8. Learn from the careers, leadership styles and decisions of successful Nigerian and global managers.

## How you are assessed

- Expect a test and an examination, mostly objective questions, plus possible short quizzes during the semester.
- Expect definitions, lists, theories and who proposed them, Nigerian examples and facts about the managers studied.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all ten.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
