## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Explain the meaning and scope of finance, the finance function (investment, financing and dividend decisions) and financial management as a tool for planning and control.
2. Compare profit maximisation and wealth maximisation, and explain stakeholders, the agency problem and agency costs.
3. Distinguish risk from uncertainty, name the types of risk, and measure risk with expected value, standard deviation and the coefficient of variation.
4. Calculate simple and compound interest, present and terminal values, annuities and perpetuities, and work with arithmetic and geometric progressions.
5. Calculate and interpret liquidity, activity, profitability, gearing and market ratios, and state the limits of ratio analysis.
6. Describe the money and capital markets, primary and secondary markets, the stock market, financial institutions and the sources of finance.
7. Compare the forms of business firm and explain limited liability.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT), with some theory questions. Expect definitions, lists, calculations and Nigerian institutions.
- Calculations come from Topics 3, 4 and 5. Practise the formulas until you can do them quickly.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all seven.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for worked examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
