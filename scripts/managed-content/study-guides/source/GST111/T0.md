## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Listen effectively in lectures and take notes that capture the essence of what is said.
2. Describe the organs of speech and identify the consonants, vowels and diphthongs of English with their symbols.
3. Divide words into syllables, place stress correctly and use the intonation tunes of English.
4. Read efficiently, using skimming, scanning and study reading, and avoid the common reading faults.
5. Answer comprehension questions and write a summary or synopsis.
6. Use study-reading methods such as SQ3R and explain how memory works.
7. Write a well-formed paragraph with a clear topic sentence, unity and coherence.
8. Write the main types of essay and punctuate correctly.
9. Build vocabulary and explain how English words are formed.
10. Identify word classes, phrases, clauses and sentence types, and avoid common sentence errors.
11. Use the library, its resources and its retrieval tools, and search electronic sources well.
12. Avoid plagiarism and cite sources in APA, Harvard and MLA style.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT). Questions test definitions, lists and counts, examples, symbols and the exact wording used in this course.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all fifteen.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for the full picture and examples.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question.
- Then practise. Each topic ends with a link to its practice questions, with an explanation for every answer.
