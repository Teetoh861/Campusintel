## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Explain what banks do, how they create credit, and how the Nigerian financial system is organised.
2. Describe the evolution, functions and forms of money.
3. Trace the history of banking in Nigeria, from the colonial banks to today's recapitalisation.
4. Explain the role, functions and monetary policy tools of the Central Bank of Nigeria.
5. Describe the types of banks and other financial institutions in Nigeria, and the role of the NDIC.
6. Explain the money market, its institutions and instruments, and work simple money market calculations.
7. Describe how a clearing bank is organised and how cheques are cleared.
8. Explain bills of exchange, cheques and the main methods of payment, including electronic payments.
9. State the rights and duties of banker and customer.
10. Explain the banking services for exporters and importers.
11. Apply the principles of lending and explain the forms of security.
12. Read a bank's balance sheet and interpret a customer's accounts.
13. Explain how banks balance liquidity, profitability and safety.
14. Describe the work of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria.

## How you are assessed

- Expect objective questions: definitions, lists, features and functions, dates and Nigerian institutions, plus some short calculations.
- Banking rules and figures change. This guide gives the current position as of 2026, and the older figures that are still examined, each with its date.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all fifteen.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
