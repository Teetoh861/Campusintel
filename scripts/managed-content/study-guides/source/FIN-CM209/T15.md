## Topic 15: The Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN)

**What you must know:** what the CIBN is and how it grew from a 1963 local centre to a chartered body; the laws behind it (Decree No. 12 of 1990, now the CIBN Act, 2007); its objectives and functions, and what it does NOT do; its Governing Council, Registrar and disciplinary system; membership categories, grades and letters; the offence of practising without membership; its link with the Bankers' Committee.

### Lesson 15.1: What the CIBN is and how it began

**Must know**

- **CIBN:** the **professional body for banking practitioners** in Nigeria. It sets the standards of knowledge and skill for persons entering the banking profession, conducts professional banking examinations and promotes ethics among bankers. It is **not** a regulator, a deposit insurer or a bank.
- It was **primarily established by its own members**, then backed by law.

**Path of development (learn the order):**

1. **1963:** bankers in Lagos set up a **local centre of the Institute of Bankers, London** (the Lagos Local Centre, resolved by 124 bankers on 28 November 1963).
2. **1973:** the **Nigerian Institute of Bankers** was established; on 15 December **1976** it was incorporated as a **company limited by guarantee**.
3. **1990:** it obtained **chartered status** by **Act (Decree) No. 12 of 1990** and became the **Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria**.
4. **2007:** the 1990 law was **repealed and replaced** by the **Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria Act, 2007** (signed 11 April 2007). This is the law that governs the CIBN today.

Short form: **Local centre of the Institute of Bankers, London -> Nigerian Institute of Bankers -> CIBN.**

**Explain more**

- Traps: chartered status did **not** come from the CBN Act 1958, the Banking Ordinance 1952 or BOFIA 1991; it came from **Decree No. 12 of 1990**. The current law is the **CIBN Act, 2007**, not the NDIC Act 2023, CAMA 2020 or BOFIA 2020 (BOFIA 2020 governs the licensing and regulation of banks, which is the CBN's job).
- Before 1990 the body was the **Nigerian Institute of Bankers**, not the "Institute of Banking and Finance", the "Nigerian Bankers' Association" or a "Banking Council". It was a **company limited by guarantee**, not a public limited company, a cooperative or a CBN corporation.
- The 1963 body was a **local centre of the London Institute**, not a chapter of the American Bankers Association, a CBN branch or the clearing house.
- The Institute's home is **Bankers House, Victoria Island, Lagos**.

### Lesson 15.2: Objectives, functions and what the CIBN does not do

**Must know**

**3 objectives** for which the CIBN was set up and backed by the 1990 Act:

1. **Promote banking education** in Nigeria.
2. **Uphold and ensure the observance of professional ethics and tradition** in the banking industry.
3. **Promote the general advancement of banking practice** in its branches, facilitate good rapport between bankers on matters of common interest, keep abreast of the latest developments in banking, and **liaise with government in promoting and regulating the banking industry**.

**4 functions under the CIBN Act, 2007 (section 3):**

1. **Determine the standards of knowledge and skill** to be attained by persons seeking to become members of the banking profession.
2. **Maintain registers** of members and of corporate members.
3. **Conduct professional examinations** and award certificates.
4. **Promote and ensure the observance of ethical standards and professionalism** among banking practitioners.

**4 features** through which it promotes professionalism among its members:

1. **Professional qualifications** through curriculum-based, rigorous training and skills development (examinations).
2. **High ethical standards**, integrity and objectivity.
3. **Codes of conduct** (rules and regulations) designed to **enforce self-regulation and compliance**.
4. **A system of recognition for merit:** honorary awards for distinguished service or outstanding contributions to banking knowledge.

**What the CIBN does NOT do** (each belongs to another body):

| Activity | Who does it |
| --- | --- |
| Granting banking licences; supervising banks | CBN (under BOFIA 2020) |
| Issuing currency; fixing the Monetary Policy Rate | CBN |
| Open market operations; dealing in treasury bills | CBN |
| Insuring bank deposits | NDIC |

**Explain more**

- "NOT a role" questions: the odd one out is always a **regulatory or central-bank power** (licensing banks, open market operations, MPR, currency) or **deposit insurance**. Liaising with government on regulation **is** an objective; actually licensing banks is **not**.
- How does the CIBN mainly promote professionalism? Through **rigorous professional examinations and training, codes of conduct, and recognition of distinguished members**.
- Codes of conduct do not guarantee profits, fix interest rates or replace CBN supervision; their benefit is **self-regulation and compliance**.
- Example: Chiamaka, a graduate trainee in a Lagos bank, who wants professional banking examinations leading to a recognised qualification registers with the **CIBN**, not the Nigerian Exchange, SEC or NDIC.
- **Bankers' Committee:** the regular meeting of the CBN with the chief executives of banks, **presided over (chaired) by the Governor of the CBN**. It is the apex of the banking profession. The CIBN and the **Financial Institutions Training Centre (FITC)** are its "twin institutions"; the CBN contributes **50%** of their budgets. The FITC is a training centre; it does not conduct the professional examinations.

### Lesson 15.3: Governance and discipline

**Must know**

- **Governing Council:** responsible for the **administration and general management** of the Institute's affairs and its policy. Chaired by the **President**. Members include the CBN Governor or his representative, bank chief executives, elected members, past Presidents and government and education representatives; the **Registrar is its Secretary**. Members serve two-year terms.
- **President:** a Fellow elected at the annual general meeting for **two years**; chairs the Council; deputised by the First Vice-President.
- **Registrar:** appointed by the Council; the **Chief Executive** of the Institute; **keeps the registers**; **Secretary to the Governing Council**.

**Disciplinary system (3 steps):**

1. **Investigating Panel:** conducts the **preliminary investigation** of an alleged breach of the code of conduct and decides whether the case should go to the Tribunal. It has 5 members.
2. **Disciplinary Tribunal:** **hears the case** and decides whether the member is guilty of unprofessional conduct. Chaired by the **Second Vice-President**.
3. **Council confirmation:** the Tribunal's direction takes effect only when **confirmed by the Council**.

Order: **Investigating Panel -> Disciplinary Tribunal -> confirmation by the Council.**

- **Sanctions:** a **reprimand**, or an order to the Registrar to **strike the member's name off the register**. **Striking off is the maximum sanction.**

**Explain more**

- A member may be sanctioned for unprofessional conduct, for a conviction the Tribunal finds incompatible with being a banker, or for registration obtained by fraud.
- The member may appeal to the **Federal High Court** within 28 days. The court is an appeal route, not the first step.
- Traps: the Tribunal cannot imprison anyone or revoke a bank's licence (licences are the CBN's). The Bankers' Committee and the CBN board play no part in CIBN discipline. The Registrar (not the President or Governor) is the Chief Executive.

### Lesson 15.4: Membership, grades and the law on practising

**Must know**

**2 broad categories of membership:** **corporate members** and **individual members**.

- **Corporate members:** institutions such as the **CBN, NDIC, banks** (including **microfinance banks**, mortgage banks and development banks) and discount houses. Corporate members must get their staff registered.
- **Individual grades:** Ordinary, Student, Graduate, **Associate**, **Honorary Senior Member**, Honorary Fellow, **Fellow**. There is **no "Principal Member"**.

| Letters | Grade |
| --- | --- |
| ACIB | Associate |
| FCIB | Fellow |
| HCIB | Honorary Senior Member |

- **Chartered banker:** a person who has **passed the professional examinations and been elected an Associate or Fellow** of the Institute.
- **Technical heads:** nobody may be employed or appointed to **head a technical department of a bank** (e.g. Treasury) unless he is a **member of the CIBN with valid, current registration**.
- **Offence:** a non-member who **practises or holds himself out as a professional banker for reward commits an offence**.

**Explain more**

- A microfinance bank can be a **corporate** member; a retired manager, a new cashier or a banking student would be an **individual** member, never a corporate one.
- ACIB is the Associate (the professional qualification earned by examination); FCIB is the senior Fellow grade; HCIB is honorary, not a "Holder of a Certificate in Banking".
- Being a chartered banker depends on **examinations plus election**, not on years of service, a directorship or a degree alone.
- Penalties: an individual offender faces up to **two years' imprisonment**, a fine of not less than **₦20,000**, or both; a body corporate faces a fine of not less than **₦200,000**. Making false statements to obtain registration is also an offence.
- The Registrar may remove a member who has not paid subscriptions for over one year.

### One-minute recap

- CIBN = professional body for bankers: standards, examinations, ethics. Not a regulator, insurer or bank.
- History: 1963 local centre of the Institute of Bankers, London -> Nigerian Institute of Bankers (company limited by guarantee, 1976) -> chartered by Decree No. 12 of 1990 -> now the CIBN Act, 2007.
- 3 objectives: banking education; professional ethics and tradition; advancement of banking practice and liaison with government.
- 4 Act functions: standards of knowledge and skill; registers; examinations and certificates; ethics and professionalism.
- Licensing banks, OMO, MPR, currency = CBN; deposit insurance = NDIC.
- Governing Council manages; Registrar = Chief Executive and Council Secretary.
- Discipline: Investigating Panel -> Disciplinary Tribunal -> Council confirmation; maximum sanction = striking off.
- Members: corporate and individual; ACIB Associate, FCIB Fellow, HCIB Honorary Senior Member.
- Technical department heads must be registered members; holding out as a banker without membership is an offence.
- Bankers' Committee is chaired by the CBN Governor.
