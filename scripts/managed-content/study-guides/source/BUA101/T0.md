## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Explain what businesses do, the environments they operate in and their major functional areas.
2. Describe economic systems, demand and supply, and how government and economic measures affect business.
3. Explain international trade, trade barriers and the forms of international business.
4. Apply ethics and corporate social responsibility to business decisions.
5. Compare the forms of business ownership and explain corporate governance, mergers and alliances.
6. Explain entrepreneurship, small-business start-up and financing, and franchising.
7. Describe the roles, functions and skills of managers.
8. Explain production systems, value chains, supply chains and quality.
9. Apply the main theories of employee motivation.
10. Describe e-business models and the business communication process.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT), with some theory questions. Expect definitions, lists, counts, Nigerian institutions and examples.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all ten.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
