## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Explain what statistics is, the types of data and scales of measurement, and how data are collected and sampled.
2. Organise data into tables and frequency distributions and present them in charts and graphs.
3. Calculate and interpret the mean, median, mode and other averages, and quartiles, deciles and percentiles.
4. Calculate and interpret the range, quartile deviation, mean deviation, variance, standard deviation and coefficient of variation.
5. Measure the shape of a distribution with coefficients of skewness and kurtosis.
6. Apply the rules of probability, including conditional probability, Bayes' theorem and counting rules.
7. Find the expected value and variance of a random variable.
8. Calculate binomial, Poisson, uniform, exponential and normal probabilities.

## How you are assessed

- Expect objective questions: definitions and classifications, and many short calculations.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all eight, and work through every worked example with a calculator.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for worked examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
