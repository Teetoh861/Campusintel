## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Define economics, explain scarcity and choice, and use opportunity cost and the production possibility frontier.
2. Tell positive from normative statements and microeconomics from macroeconomics, and read and interpret economic graphs.
3. Name the main areas of specialisation and the major schools of economic thought.
4. Explain demand, supply and equilibrium, and solve for equilibrium price and quantity.
5. Calculate and interpret price, income and cross elasticities, and show who bears a tax.
6. Explain how consumers choose, using utility and indifference curves.
7. Explain production, costs and diminishing returns, economies of scale and the rewards of factors.
8. Apply partial equilibrium analysis to taxes, subsidies and price controls.
9. Compare market structures and explain efficiency, welfare and market failure.

## How you are assessed

- The exam is objective (CBT). Expect definitions, curve shifts and many short computations: equilibrium, elasticity, costs, utility and profit.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all ten.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for the worked examples and the traps.
- Work every computation yourself with a pen before you read the answer.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the practice question, then do that topic's practice questions.
