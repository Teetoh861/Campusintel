## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Identify and evaluate marketing communication techniques, and the principles and theories behind each.
2. Explain the marketing communication process and its models.
3. Plan marketing communications and choose the right mix of advertising, sales promotion, public relations, personal selling and digital tools.
4. Explain the role of the source, message, receiver, medium and opinion leaders.
5. Explain the forms of business communication and the communication process.
6. Explain the place of social media, word-of-mouth and digital marketing in modern marketing communication.
7. Apply marketing communication concepts, principles and theories to solve marketing communication problems.

## How you are assessed

- Assignments: 10%. Mid-term test: 20%. End-of-semester examination: 70%.
- The exam is objective (CBT), with some theory questions. Expect definitions, lists, counts, models, authors and short applications to Nigerian businesses.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all eight.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
