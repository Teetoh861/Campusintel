## Course outcomes

By the end of this course you should be able to:

1. Discuss the meaning of leadership and governance.
2. Identify the basic concepts and elements of governance.
3. Explain governance in the public sector and its challenges.
4. Identify gaps in public governance and initiatives to improve them.
5. Identify traditional and contemporary leadership theories and styles.
6. Apply moral philosophies to leadership and governance problems.
7. Explain how citizens and civil society can be engaged in governance reform.
8. Take an active part in public sector reform in Nigeria.

## How you are assessed

- Expect objective questions, with possible short theory questions. Expect definitions, lists, theories and who proposed them, and Nigerian applications.
- Every topic in this guide is examinable. Read all eight.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic is split into short lessons of about 5 minutes.
- Read **Must know** first. It is the minimum you need. Read **Explain more** for examples and the traps.
- Finish each topic with the one-minute recap and the theory question, then do that topic's practice questions.
